# Open-Source Model Strategy v1

Open-source/open-weight does not mean compute-free. Local models use Jennie's hardware or a self-hosted server. Hosted open models may still charge inference fees.

Preferred use:
- `classify_blocker`
- `summarize_status`
- `draft_task`
- `draft_improvement_suggestion`
- `draft_hire_request`
- `draft_business_doc`
- `draft_product_description`
- `draft_seo_tags`
- `summarize_customer_segment`
- `create_test_suggestion`

Boundaries:
- Local/open models may handle public or business-internal work after approval.
- Sensitive data remains blocked unless an authority request is approved.
- No model receives provider tokens, full bank details, EIN, or high-authority fields by default.
- No model can grant itself tools, money movement, publishing, sending, syncing, deleting, or external ordering authority.
