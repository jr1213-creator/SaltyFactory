# Marketing Command Center v1

Marketing Command Center is a native manual/export-ready campaign operating system.

## Fully Functional v1

- `/studio/marketing-command-center`: real counts from shared-kernel records.
- `/studio/marketing-campaigns`: campaign create/list/detail/edit.
- `/studio/marketing-command-center/launch-campaign`: guided workflow that creates a campaign packet.
- Campaign drafts stored in `campaigns`.
- Channel drafts stored in `campaign_channels`.
- Campaign Proof Pack stored in `export_packages` with `package_type=proof_pack`.
- No-Ad Growth Plan stored in `export_packages` with `package_type=growth_plan`.
- Ad Readiness Score stored in `readiness_scores` with `score_type=ad`.
- UTM links generated server-side and stored in `utm_links`.
- Approval queue stored in `approvals`.
- Asset specs stored in `assets`.
- Research items stored in `source_records`.

## Manual / Export Ready

- Pinterest Pin Factory
- Social Draft Queue
- Email Draft Studio
- Google Ads Draft Studio
- Meta Ads Draft Studio
- Campaign Asset Studio
- Search/AEO/GEO readiness report
- Voice-of-Market Research Board
- Creative Intelligence Swipe File

These features create editable, persisted records for owner review and manual export. They do not publish, send, submit, launch ads, or spend money.

## Safe Claims

Safe: SaltyFactory prepares proof-backed campaign packets, ad drafts, no-ad growth plans, UTM links, and owner approval queues.

Not safe: SaltyFactory runs ads automatically, sends campaigns, guarantees rankings, guarantees AI search visibility, or publishes social posts.
