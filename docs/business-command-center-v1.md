# Business Command Center v1

Status: functional v1 for persisted business decision support.

Implemented:
- `/studio/business`
- profile-derived readiness score
- persisted goals, mantras, unit economics, opportunities, decision memos, forecasts, experiments, channel readiness, authority requests, documents, bank records, and print packets
- Make Me Look Legit bundle

Boundaries:
- Not accounting, tax, legal, investment, or banking advice.
- No ad spend, live price changes, publish, sync, email send, or external form submission.
- Unknown inputs create unknown/blocker states.

Future:
- Provider-fed order/revenue/customer metrics.
- Rich portfolio optimization.
